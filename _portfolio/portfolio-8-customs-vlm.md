## Project Overview

During my internship at the General Department of Customs and Excise of Cambodia (GCDE), I worked on improving trade document processing through VLM-based OCR solutions for complex customs paperwork.

### Key Contributions

- Developed and evaluated OCR pipelines for logistics and customs documents
- Compared and benchmarked several OCR and document understanding models
- Improved document parsing for invoices, packing lists, and airway bills
- Optimized deployment for local environments using vLLM on limited GPU hardware

### Key Outcomes

- Built a more robust OCR workflow for real-world customs documents
- Improved handling of low-resolution and structurally complex trade documents
- Contributed to a practical AI solution for operational document processing

### Technologies Used

- Python
- vLLM
- Vision-Language Models (VLMs)
- OCR frameworks such as dot.mocr, PaddleOCR, IBM Docling, MinerU, Marker, and GLM-OCR
- PyTorch and Transformers
- Pandas, NumPy, and Matplotlib
